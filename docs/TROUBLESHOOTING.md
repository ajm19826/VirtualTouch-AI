# Troubleshooting

## Cursor Does Not Move

1. Open Diagnostics.
2. Verify Camera permission.
3. Verify Accessibility permission.
4. Select Test cursor movement.
5. If the test does not move the cursor, re-enable Accessibility access.
6. Restart VirtualTouch AI.

## Finger Is Not Detected

- Improve lighting without glare.
- Move your hand closer to the camera.
- Keep the index fingertip visible.
- Avoid placing the hand against a background with low contrast.
- Stop and restart tracking after changing camera distance.

## Camera Unavailable

Disconnect and reconnect an external webcam. Close another app that has exclusive control of the camera, then restart VirtualTouch AI.

## Clicks Repeat or Do Not Trigger

- Increase or decrease Click sensitivity in Settings.
- Increase Click cooldown to prevent duplicate actions.
- Enable dwell-to-click as a controlled alternative.
- Use the Diagnostics Test left click button to verify OS input delivery.

## Voice Commands Do Not Fire

- Enable Say Click.
- Verify Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions.
- Speak Click, Left, or Stop clearly near the microphone.
- Keep tracking active for voice control.
