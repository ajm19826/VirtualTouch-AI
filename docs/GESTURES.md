# Gestures and Clicks

## Cursor Tracking

Point your index finger toward the screen. The cursor follows the visible fingertip first. When a fingertip is hidden, arm and wrist landmarks provide fallback estimation.

## Click Methods

- Wrist jolt
- Arm extension or stop/reversal
- Flick
- Dwell-to-click, when enabled
- Voice: Click or Left
- Test controls in Diagnostics

## Drag

Hold a confirmed pinch for five consecutive frames, move your finger, and release the pinch to end the drag.

## Swipe

Move your wrist through five tracked positions. Directional movement may scroll the active application.

## Supported Gesture Labels

Point, open palm, fist, thumb gestures, peace sign, pinch, multi-finger poses, L shape, call me, OK sign, finger gun, wave, directional swipes, clap, and stop hand.

Gesture recognition requires stable landmarks and uses cooldowns to reduce accidental activation.
