# Calibration Guide

## Prepare

- Sit facing the built-in or external webcam.
- Keep your shoulders, arm, wrist, and index finger visible when possible.
- Use even lighting.
- Keep the camera fixed during calibration and use.

## Run Calibration

1. Open Calibration.
2. Select Start or Recalibrate.
3. The app opens a full-screen calibration surface.
4. Point at the target position.
5. Capture five samples for the first target.
6. Continue through all nine targets.
7. Finish to save the profile locally.

Calibration targets cover:

- Top left, top center, top right
- Middle left, center, middle right
- Bottom left, bottom center, bottom right

Recalibrate when changing seating position, display setup, camera angle, distance, or dominant hand.
